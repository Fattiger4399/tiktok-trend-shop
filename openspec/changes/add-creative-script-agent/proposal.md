## Why

The product needs to convert research cards into usable short-form selling scripts without losing factual grounding. This change adds a creative script agent that generates multiple TikTok-ready variants with controlled claims, structure, and revision history.

## What Changes

- Add structured script generation from approved product research cards.
- Generate multiple variants by duration, audience, tone, hook style, and language.
- Produce hooks, scene beats, voiceover, captions, hashtags, CTA, and compliance notes.
- Validate scripts against source-backed product claims and risk flags.
- Track prompt versions, generated variants, and user revisions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `creative-script-agent`: Structured AI generation, validation, and revision of TikTok selling scripts from product research cards.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- Depends on `app-foundation` and `product-intelligence`.
- Affects prompt templates, generation jobs, script storage, validation rules, and creative review inputs.

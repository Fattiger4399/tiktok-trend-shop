## Why

AI-generated product content needs human review before export or publishing because product claims, copyright, platform policy, and quality issues can create account and business risk. This change adds a review queue and export package workflow.

## What Changes

- Add review states for product research, scripts, storyboards, and rendered videos.
- Add a compliance checklist for claim support, policy-sensitive categories, copyright, AI disclosure, and quality.
- Add reviewer comments, rejection reasons, and revision routing.
- Add export packages containing video files, captions, hashtags, source evidence, and metadata.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `review-export-workflow`: Human review, compliance checklist, revision routing, and export packaging for generated product videos.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- Depends on `app-foundation`, `product-intelligence`, `creative-script-agent`, and `ai-video-generation`.
- Affects review states, reviewer UI or API surfaces, compliance metadata, export asset generation, and downstream publishing readiness.

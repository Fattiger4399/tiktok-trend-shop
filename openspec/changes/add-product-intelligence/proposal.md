## Why

Raw trend data is not enough to decide which products deserve content production. This change turns collected source signals into ranked product opportunities and detailed product research cards that downstream creative modules can trust.

## What Changes

- Add trend opportunity scoring for candidate products.
- Add explainable score components for growth, demand, competition, economics, content potential, and risk.
- Add product research cards with selling points, audience, objections, proof, competitor context, and compliance warnings.
- Add score and research versioning so generated creative assets can be traced to the inputs used.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `product-intelligence`: Product opportunity scoring and research card generation from normalized trend data.

### Modified Capabilities
- None.

## Impact

- Depends on `app-foundation` and `trend-source-ingestion`.
- Affects scoring services, product research generation, evidence extraction, compliance risk detection, and product candidate views.
